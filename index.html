Adam Bouyamourn

I am an Assistant Professor and Faculty Fellow at the Center for Data Science at New York University.

I am a methodologist. My research uses optimization to design efficient experiments, and studies the social consequences of AI systems. (See experimental site selection under distribution shift and algorithmic lending.)

Before NYU, I was a Postdoctoral Research Associate in Politics, Statistics and Machine Learning at Princeton University, where I worked with Arthur Spirling. I completed my PhD in Political Science at UC Berkeley, advised by Thad Dunning, with Kirk Bansak, Erin Hartman, Avi Feller and Peng Ding on my committee.

Before graduate school I was a journalist, at The Guardian and The National. I am British and Moroccan, and a lifelong chorister.
