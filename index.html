Adam Bouyamourn

I am an Assistant Professor and Faculty Fellow at the Center for Data Science at New York University.

I study the social implications of AI systems, and develop new methods for researchers in the social sciences.

Before NYU, I was a Postdoctoral Research Associate in Politics, Statistics and Machine Learning at Princeton University, where I worked with Arthur Spirling. I completed my PhD in Political Science at UC Berkeley.

Before graduate school I was a journalist, at The Guardian (London) and The National (Abu Dhabi).
I am British and Moroccan, and a lifelong chorister.
